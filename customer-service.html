Frequently asked questions

How do I add an item to my cart?

Open a product and click Add to cart. Your cart count updates automatically.

Does my cart survive a page refresh?

Yes. This demo stores your cart in your browser's localStorage.

How do I remove an item?

Open the Cart page and use the Remove button beside the item.

Can I create an account?

Yes. Use the Sign In page. This demo stores account information locally in your browser and does not send it to a server.
